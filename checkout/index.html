YOUR PROGRESS

60 seconds · +20 per checkout · no run cap

Finish the target on a double in one visit. A successful checkout earns +20 seconds and a new target. After three unsuccessful darts, retry the original target. Bust ends the run immediately. First 5 checkouts: one-dart targets. Next 5: two-dart targets. After 10: increasingly difficult mixed targets. Impossible visits reset automatically with three fresh darts. Clock starts on the first throw, keeps running between visits and in the background, with no run cap. The last 20 seconds trigger a ticking clock.

CONTROLS

Tap to nudge. Hold to move.
Hold 0.25 seconds for 2.2× speed.
WASD / arrow keys also aim.
Hold SPACE to set power.
Release to throw.

On mobile, use the D-pad and hold / release THROW.

Darts collect automatically after a visit.
The next visit starts after 1 second.
